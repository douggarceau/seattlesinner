## How to approve a listing

1. Open the source link(s) and confirm name, address, hours, cost and eligibility. Call if the page is unclear.
2. In `data/hbc/<category>.json`, set `"status": "verified"`, `"checked": "<today>"`, `"checked_by": "owner"`, and a new `review_by`.
3. Run `python3 tools/validate_hbc.py`; it must report 0 errors.

- **Wallingford Food Bank (food-0010):** a Nov 2025 neighborhood blog said it moved temporarily to 4318 Stone Way N; the official site still shows 1501 N 45th St. Call to confirm.
- **OSL Serves (food-0005):** the grab-and-go end time isn't published, so only lunch and dinner times are listed.
- **ZIP codes filled from mailing or directory addresses:** SODO Community Market, St. Mary's and Pike Market.
- **Urban Rest Stop Downtown (hyg-0001):** the address appears as both 1924 and 1925 9th Ave, and the hours appear in three versions. The listing uses LIHI's Mon–Fri 6:30am–4pm.
- **Compass Hygiene Center (hyg-0003):** its own pages give two weekday opening times, 7:00 and 8:30am. The listing uses 8:30.
- **Delridge showers (hyg-0006):** the 2026 closure list names Dec 11, but the "first Friday" rule would make it Dec 4.
- **Cost marked `unknown` (19 listings):** the provider's page doesn't say the service is free, so no free claim is made. Confirm by phone and change to `free` where true. These are 9 hygiene, 4 supplies and 6 health listings.
- **Seattle Indian Health Board (health):** its pages don't say whether non-Native patients are accepted.
- **Downtown Public Health Center (health):** the county says no one is turned away and services are "free or low cost". The clinic's own page states no fee, and the county says appointments are temporarily required.
- **ABD cash (benefits):** the $450/$570 monthly amounts have no effective date on the DSHS page. One eligibility point in the notes is unconfirmed and is not in the public text.
- **Apple Health and Basic Food (benefits):** federal rule changes are under way (work rules, 6-month renewals from Jan 1, 2027, and narrower eligibility for some noncitizens since Oct 1, 2026). Recheck before launch.
- **HEN (benefits):** DSHS says the program depends on funding and could end at any time.
