Functions

Command line

Start typing anywhere to use the command line. Enter a ticker (NVDA), a ticker plus function (NVDA DES), a function alone (IMAP), or a question after ASK or ?. Plain names work too: GOLD, OIL, BITCOIN, 10Y, YEN, NIKKEI. Arrow keys move through suggestions; Esc closes overlays.

Workspaces & layout

Pick a workspace (Default, Pre-Market, Research, Markets, Portfolio, News) from the bar under the command line, or press Alt+Shift+1 to 6. STOCKS / ₿ CRYPTO switches desks instantly (Alt+Shift+7 for crypto). Drag a panel’s bottom-right corner to resize it; double-click the corner to restore it. Every panel has two buttons in its top-right corner: – minimizes it to its title bar (+ brings it back) and □ maximizes it to the full window (❐, Esc, or a double-click on the title bar puts it back; ` over a panel does the same). The A buttons at the top set the text size, from 120% to 180%. ⟲ DEFAULT VIEW (or Alt+Shift+0, or type RESET) puts everything back.

Voice

Press 🎤 in the command line and say a ticker or function (“Nvidia”, “crypto”, “reset”), or a full question to send it to the analyst. 🎤 in the AI Analyst asks out loud, 🔊 there reads every answer aloud, and 🔊 READ reads Claude’s briefs. Voice input needs a browser with speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari) and microphone access for this page.

About the data

Sources: CNBC quote and chart services, Nasdaq (profiles, analyst targets, short interest, earnings and economic calendars), US Treasury par yield curve, Bureau of Labor Statistics, New York Fed reference rates, CNN Fear & Greed, Coinbase Exchange, and the Federal Reserve FOMC calendar. Market clocks ignore exchange holidays.
